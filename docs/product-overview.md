# Product Overview

## Product Name

Nexa Retail

## Product Type

Retail & Café Management Platform

## Vision

Provide a simple, reliable, and modern system that helps supermarket and café employees manage daily operations without requiring advanced computer skills.

The system should reduce:

- forgotten orders
- inventory mistakes
- cash discrepancies
- supplier-account confusion
- customer-credit confusion
- manual reporting
- expired stock
- unnecessary work when receiving supplier deliveries

# Major Modules

## 1. Authentication & Authorization

Roles and permissions.

## 2. POS

Retail checkout and payments.

## 3. Cashier Shifts

Opening cash, transactions, closing reconciliation.

## 4. Inventory

Products, stock, stock movements, expiry, adjustments.

## 5. Suppliers

Supplier accounts, purchases, payments, balances.

## 6. Customers

Customer profiles, credit sales, payments, balances.

## 7. Accounting

Expenses, receivables, payables, reports.

## 8. Café

Orders and barista workflow.

## 9. Printing

Receipts and printable business documents. Production Arabic RTL thermal receipts (58/80mm) for sales, café orders (with linked invoice), and customer payments — server-derived from stored transactions, permission-controlled, reprint-safe.

## 10. Online Store

Public product catalog, cart, checkout, orders.

## 11. Delivery

Delivery status and order fulfillment.

## 12. Reports

Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, product, cashier, supplier, customer, and payment reports.

# Design Principles

The system should feel:

- modern
- trustworthy
- fast
- calm
- practical
- easy to learn
- visually distinctive

It should not feel like:

- a generic admin dashboard
- a template
- an AI-generated UI
- an overly decorative SaaS landing page

Operational screens should prioritize speed and clarity.

Customer-facing screens should prioritize trust, product discovery, and mobile usability.
